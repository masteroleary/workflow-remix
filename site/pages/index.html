WorkflowRemix is a private desktop app for the images, video and audio you generate with ComfyUI. Browse the library, open any file to see the workflow inside it, and remix it with a new prompt, seed or model. Your library never leaves your computer.

How it works

WorkflowRemix generates nothing itself and contains no ComfyUI code. It talks to the ComfyUI you already run, with your models and your custom nodes.

  1. Point it at your ComfyUI

    On first launch you choose where your data lives and give the address ComfyUI answers on. That is the whole setup.

  2. Open any file

    ComfyUI writes the workflow into what it saves. WorkflowRemix reads it back, so every picture remembers how it was made.

  3. Change one thing and run

    The run goes to your ComfyUI queue. Results appear as thumbnails while they finish, ready to keep, favourite or remix again.

Private by design

A media library is personal. The app is built so that it stays on your computer, and so that you can see exactly what leaves it.

Privacy and security in full

  • Your library never leaves the machine. Media is served straight off your disk. Nothing phones home.
  • It runs only in its own window. The app answers nothing but that window. A browser pointed at its address gets a page saying where the app is.
  • You choose where the data lives. The first launch asks, and says whether the folder you picked is probably indexed by system search.
  • Nothing is cached on disk by default. Thumbnails and media are not kept by the window unless you change the setting.
  • A password and a safe mode, if you want them. Lock the whole app, and hide files whose prompt matches your own word list on a shared screen.
  • One background request, and it is named. The update check tells GitHub your IP address and that a copy is checking. Nothing about your media, prompts or settings.

Yours to erase, on your terms

A private library is yours, and it should be yours to remove — quickly, and without special tools or a trip to a command line. WorkflowRemix wipes its own data on demand, and gives you a way out if you are ever made to unlock it.

How secure wipe works

  • Secure wipe, in one click. Erase your whole library, ComfyUI's input, output and cache, and the app's own prompts, rules and workflows together. Back it up first if you want to, or not at all. No admin rights and no scheduled task — it runs in the app.
  • A panic password. Set a second password on the lock screen that erases instead of letting you in. If someone forces you to open the app, it opens onto nothing. Off until you set it, and it takes no confirmation and keeps no backup — because that is the point.
  • Clears Windows' own trail too. Optionally clears the Recent-files list and jump lists Windows keeps, and shows you how to clear cached thumbnails with Windows' own Disk Cleanup.
  • Ready to sell or hand on. Turn on BitLocker or FileVault and the whole disk is unreadable without your key. The app points you at the operating system's own steps to reset the machine cleanly before it changes hands — it never reaches past its own data itself.

For adults, within the law

WorkflowRemix is for lawful use by adults (18 and over). You are responsible for every prompt, model and LoRA you run, and for what you make and share. The app adds one safety check in front of the ComfyUI you run — something ComfyUI does not do on its own — so the clearest misuse is refused before a render ever starts.

Acceptable Use Policy

  • Sexual content involving minors is never allowed, in any form: real or fictional, photoreal or drawn. Neither are intimate images of real people made without their consent.
  • Every run is checked before it reaches ComfyUI. A run whose prompt, model name or LoRA name describes a minor sexually is refused. An ordinary picture of a child, or of a school, runs.
  • Nothing switches the check off. It is a word check and not an image classifier: it stops the run that asks, and cannot see what a model draws unasked.
