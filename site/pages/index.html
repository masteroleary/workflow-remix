How it works

WorkflowRemix generates nothing itself and contains no ComfyUI code. It talks to the ComfyUI you already run, with your models and your custom nodes.

  1. Install it, point it at your ComfyUI

    It installs like any other app: no Python, no command line. The first launch asks where your data lives and where ComfyUI is, then checks your ComfyUI and says what it is missing. It updates itself from then on.

  2. Open any file

    ComfyUI writes the workflow into what it saves. WorkflowRemix reads it back, so every picture remembers how it was made, and what it saves is exactly what ran.

  3. Change one thing and run

    The run goes to your ComfyUI queue. Results appear as they finish, ready to keep, favourite or remix again.

Private by design

A media library is personal. The app is built so that it stays on your computer, and so that you can see exactly what leaves it.

Privacy and security in full {{icon:play-circle-outline}}Watch it

  • Your library never leaves the machine. Media is served straight off your disk. Nothing phones home.
  • It runs only in its own window, and loads nothing from the internet. The window refuses any request that is not to the app itself.
  • Every privacy choice, one question at a time. A short setup walks through the password, caching, blur, safe mode, search indexing and secure wipe.
  • Blur that comes back by itself. One tap blurs every picture, and whenever the app goes out of sight it is back on when you return.
  • A shield, and a demo mode. Hide every file your word list or an NSFW tag flags. Demo mode also hides the models, LoRAs and workflows tagged NSFW, so you can show the app to anyone.
  • Nothing is cached on disk by default, and the app warns you if system search can read your folders.
  • One background request, and it is named. The update check tells GitHub your IP address and that a copy is checking. No account, no licence server, no analytics, no crash reports.

Yours to erase, on your terms

A private library should be yours to remove, quickly, without special tools or a command line. WorkflowRemix wipes its own data on demand, and gives you a way out if you are ever made to unlock it.

How secure wipe works

  • Secure wipe, part by part. Tick what goes: your library, ComfyUI's input, output and cache, the prompt library, Studios' projects, the job list, logs and more. Back it up first or not. No admin rights; it runs in the app.
  • A panic password. A second password on the lock screen that erases instead of letting you in. If you are made to open the app, it opens onto nothing.
  • Clears Windows' own trail too, if you ask: the Recent-files list and jump lists.
  • Ready to sell or hand on. The app points you to the operating system's own encryption and reset, and never reaches past its own data itself.

For adults, within the law

WorkflowRemix is for lawful use by adults (18 and over). You are responsible for every prompt, model and LoRA you run, and for what you make and share. The app adds one safety check in front of the ComfyUI you run — something ComfyUI does not do on its own — so the clearest misuse is refused before a render ever starts.

Acceptable Use Policy

  • Sexual content involving minors is never allowed, in any form: real or fictional, photoreal or drawn. Neither are intimate images of real people made without their consent.
  • Every run is checked before it reaches ComfyUI. A run whose prompt, model name or LoRA name describes a minor sexually is refused. An ordinary picture of a child, or of a school, runs.
  • Nothing switches the check off. It is a word check and not an image classifier: it stops the run that asks, and cannot see what a model draws unasked.
