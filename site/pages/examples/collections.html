Starter collections

A new install starts with a prompt library filed in five collections. This page shows what is in them, and what one prompt becomes in each: a scene in four of them, and a logo in the fifth.

One prompt

The library has a shelf called Showcase with a single row in it, and the rule [showcase] points at that row. Its text is six keywords and nothing else:

[style], [character], [expression], [action], [scene], [lighting]

Each keyword is answered from the collection chosen in the Collection drop-down at the top of the rules panel. Change the drop-down and every keyword is answered again, with no rule edited. The colours below are the ones the app's own preview uses: each stretch of text is painted in the colour of the keyword that put it there.

Logo Design

The one collection whose picture is not a scene. It has a Showcase row of its own, so here [showcase] is answered by the collection itself, and names five keywords of its own:

[style], [subject], [layout], [palette], [background], professional brand identity design, vector artwork, crisp edges, high contrast

minimalist flat vector logo, simple geometric shapes, clean lines, solid colors, a fox head, a single centered icon mark with no lettering, orange and charcoal color palette, on a plain white background, professional brand identity design, vector artwork, crisp edges, high contrast

Out of the box it has three styles ticked, Minimal Flat, Vintage Badge and Mascot, and one of everything else, so one click on Run queues one fox mark in three styles. It is the same fall back read the other way: a collection overrides any keyword by having the shelf, the template included.

Make your own

Collections are added, renamed and removed on the Prompts page. A new collection starts empty and borrows every keyword from General; give it a shelf and that keyword becomes its own. How collections work has the rules.
