Examples

What a new install comes with, written out, so you can see how the pieces fit before you build your own.

  • {{icon:graph-outline}}Starter workflowsThe six workflows copied into your ComfyUI on first run: what each does and what it needs.
  • {{icon:bookshelf}}Starter collectionsFour prompt collections, and one prompt answered by each of them.
  • {{icon:lightbulb-on-outline}}Prompt recipesShort patterns for variations, two figures in one picture, and model comparisons.

These are starting points

The workflows name the models and custom nodes their author used, and the collections are deliberately small. Both are copied into your own install once, on first run, and are yours to change from then on.
