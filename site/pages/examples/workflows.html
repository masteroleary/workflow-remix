Starter workflows

On first run WorkflowRemix copies five example workflows into your ComfyUI workflows folder and switches them on, so Remix has something to run. A few sample pictures come with them, in your Favorites.

Expect to install or swap the models

Each workflow names the models, LoRAs and custom nodes its author used. Press Enable on its card on the Workflows page and the app checks every one against your ComfyUI, then lists what is missing and which folder each file belongs in (checking a workflow). In Remix, a model your ComfyUI does not have shows as an amber pill, ready to be replaced with one you do have. Models are never downloaded for you, and each carries a licence of its own that you must follow.

Text to image

Krea2

Text to image · the simplest one

A prompt in, a picture out, in eight steps. Thirteen nodes: a model, its text encoder and VAE, a LoRA stack and a sampler. The one to start with, and the easiest to read in ComfyUI.

File
APP Krea2.json
Form
Prompt, seed, steps, guidance, size, model, LoRAs
Needs
A Krea 2 turbo diffusion model with its text encoder and VAE, and the rgthree and Easy-Use node packs

Editing a picture

Krea Image Edit

Image edit · by instruction

You give it a picture and say what to change: “make the jacket red”, “relight the scene with warm golden-hour sun”. Its prompt is an instruction and not a description, so WorkflowRemix keeps the workflow's own instruction when you switch to it, where it would otherwise carry the picture's prompt over. The picture you remix is the one it edits, and Match Input Image sizes the result to it. Its sample run turns the cartoon portrait in your Favorites into a photograph.

File
APP Krea Image Edit.json
Form
Picture to edit, instruction, seed, steps, guidance, size, model
Needs
The Krea 2 turbo model, its text encoder and VAE, the Krea 2 identity edit LoRA, and the comfyui-krea2edit node pack

Krea Image Edit by Reference

Image edit · a person into a scene

Two pictures: a scene, and a person to place into it (“place this person at the café table, holding a coffee”). The main image is the scene and the second reference is the person. Like Krea Image Edit, it keeps its own instruction when you switch to it. It ships with no sample pictures, so run it from Remix on two of your own.

File
APP Krea Image Edit by Reference.json
Form
Scene, the person's picture, instruction, seed, steps, guidance, size, model
Needs
The same as Krea Image Edit

Image to video

Minimax H3 Video

Image to video · with sound

MiniMax H3 makes the picture move and writes its soundtrack in the same pass: voices, sound effects and music together, in stereo, for clips of up to about fifteen seconds. Describe the motion and the sound in the prompt. Its sample run animates the cyberpunk portrait in your Favorites to a 1980s synthwave score.

File
APP Minimax H3 Video.json
Form
Input picture, prompt, seed, duration, model
Needs
The MiniMax H3 diffusion model, its text encoder, video and audio VAEs and turbo LoRA, and a recent ComfyUI, whose own nodes run it: no custom nodes

Wan High Low

Image to video · WAN 2.2

A still in, a short clip out. WAN 2.2 uses a pair of models, one for the high-noise steps and one for the low-noise steps, each with a LoRA stack of its own. Remix treats a high and low LoRA pair as one: add it to one stack and its partner is added to the other. Frame interpolation smooths the motion, and the clip's last frame is saved beside it as a picture, so the next clip can start where this one ends.

File
APP Wan High Low.json
Form
Input picture, prompt, seed, total and high-noise steps, guidance, frames, size, the high and low models and their LoRAs
Needs
WAN 2.2 image-to-video models (high and low noise, in GGUF form), their text encoder, CLIP vision model and VAE, a RIFE interpolation model, and the GGUF, rgthree, KJNodes, Video Helper Suite, Frame Interpolation, Easy-Use and Quality of Life Suit node packs

Using your own

Nothing about these five is special. Any workflow you save in ComfyUI appears on the Workflows page, where one tick offers it in Remix. The form is worked out from the graph, so there is nothing to convert. Changed one of these and want the original back? Restore defaults puts it back and keeps your version beside it.
