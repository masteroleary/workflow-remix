Media and jobs

Media opens a grid over everything you have made: images, video and audio, by folder, right where ComfyUI saves them. Nothing to import and nothing to file.

Watch a picture turned into a video and then trimmed, split and joined.

Where it looks

The folder name at the top opens Library: Output, ComfyUI's own output folder, so a render shows up without being copied anywhere; Input, what runs are fed from; and Favorites, everything you kept. The chips under the search box set the sort, the layout (Grid or Mason) and tile size, the page size, and filter by type or flatten every subfolder into one grid. Tiles are small copies made for the grid, so a library of full-size renders loads quickly, a phone included.

Search by name, or by prompt

The app reads the prompt written inside each generated file, the moment a run finishes. So a search for “ocean” finds every picture whose prompt mentioned it, whatever the file is called. Search can go down into every subfolder, and Tags lists every word and phrase across your prompts, to pick one and see every file that used it.

A tile's wf tag means the file carries a workflow; its picture opens the viewer, and its bar opens Remix. A video and the still saved beside it under the same name are one item, listed, moved and deleted together.

The viewer

Full screen, with swipe or the arrows to move through the folder, and a slideshow. The expand button opens a picture alone to pinch, scroll and drag around in, and Blur stays on there if it is on. The ⋯ menu has the tools:

  • Split video and Trim video: two parts at the position you choose, or one part from a start and a length. Each part keeps the clip's prompt and workflow, so it remixes like the clip.
  • Last frame: a video's final frame as a picture, so the next clip can start where this one ends.
  • Duplicate, and Remove Workflow, which takes the embedded workflow and prompt out of a picture or video before you share it.
  • Save to phone, on another device: the share sheet, after a question saying a saved copy is outside the app's privacy settings.

Joining and comparing clips

Select two or more videos and choose Merge clips to join them end to end into a new one; Frame sheet makes one picture of a clip's frames in order. The clips you picked are left as they are.

Keeping, renaming, removing

  • Favorite moves a file into your library, which is how a render becomes a kept file.
  • Upload puts pictures from the device you are using into the folder on screen, never over a file already there.
  • Selecting (or a long press on a folder) turns the bar into the selection's: Rename many files at once, Move, Favorite, Tag NSFW or Delete them together, and Merge folders into the first one you picked.

Delete means delete

The file is removed from the folder it is in, not hidden. The app's delete buttons reach every folder above, ComfyUI's own included.

Jobs

Every run you start is a job on the Jobs page: the workflow it ran, what made it this job and not its neighbour, the pictures it used, its progress and a thumbnail for each output as it finishes. The sidebar's Jobs row counts what is running and fills a ring with the job on the graphics card, on every page.

  • One list for every device. A job started on the computer is on your phone's list too, and the other way round. The newest 50 are kept.
  • Remix any job, however it ended, with its workflow and prompt, even one that never finished. A Studios job has Go to project.
  • Cancel stops it in ComfyUI, not only in the list, and keeps at it until ComfyUI lets go. A job that has gone quiet for five minutes says so.
  • A failed job says why: ComfyUI's own reason, usually a node or model your install lacks, and Report under the log sends it to support if you want.

One press can queue many jobs: several models, several files, several prompt variations. They go to ComfyUI model by model, so each checkpoint loads once, and several models share their seeds, so a side-by-side comparison is a comparison of models. Watch it in Compare models side by side.

On a shared screen

Blur in the sidebar blurs every thumbnail and the picture in the viewer, and comes back on by itself whenever the app goes out of sight. The shield leaves out every file your word list or an NSFW tag flags, and its demo mode hides the models, LoRAs and workflows tagged NSFW too. More in Privacy and security, and in the privacy video.
