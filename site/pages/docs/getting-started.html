Getting started

Install the app, answer a few questions on its first launch, and your ComfyUI output folder is a library you can search and remix.

What you need

  • A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 computer. Versions for macOS and Linux are on the way.
  • Your own ComfyUI, with the models and custom nodes you use. A Docker install works as well as a native one, and so does a ComfyUI on another computer on your network.
  • To be 18 or older, and to agree to the Acceptable Use Policy.

Install it

Get the installer from Downloads: WorkflowRemix-Setup-<version>.exe. A short wizard asks who it is for and where the program goes. Only for me, the default, needs no administrator rights and keeps updates silent. Until the installer is code-signed, Windows shows Windows protected your PC: choose More info, then Run anyway.

The first launch

The app asks its questions itself, the first time it opens. Nothing runs until you press Start WorkflowRemix.

  1. Where your data lives. This folder holds your settings, prompts and rules, Studios' projects, the job list with the full text of every prompt you run, the logs and, unless you choose otherwise, your media library. For the most privacy, pick a drive your operating system does not index for search, ideally an encrypted one. The screen says, for each folder you pick, whether it is probably indexed, and warns about a folder that syncs to a cloud service.
  2. Media library (optional). Where favourites go. Leave it empty to keep it inside the data folder.
  3. ComfyUI. The address ComfyUI answers on, and optionally its folder: the one holding its input, output and user folders (for Docker, the host side of those mounts). That is what lets the app list your workflows and outputs.
  4. The Acceptable Use Policy. Read it, tick I am 18 or older, and I agree, and press Start.

The data folder is a real decision

It cannot be moved from inside the app afterwards. A search index keeps its own copy of file names and of the text inside files, and that copy outlives the files. Accept the default and your data goes in your user profile, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\WorkflowRemix.

Connect ComfyUI

Until the app knows where ComfyUI is, it opens on a page asking for its address and folder. Once ComfyUI answers, the same page checks that ComfyUI-Manager is on and that GGUF models can load, and says how to put either right. It installs nothing itself. Instead, two buttons on every screen:

  • Copy agent request copies a short, exact request for an AI coding agent you already use (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and the like): what is missing, where your ComfyUI keeps its models, and the node packs and versions the bundled workflows are tested with.
  • Show instructions gives the same steps to follow yourself.

No ComfyUI yet? I don't have ComfyUI writes the request for installing the Windows portable build for your graphics card.

From then on the sidebar's ComfyUI row shows its memory use, opens ComfyUI's own page, and restarts it. If ComfyUI stops answering, a red row says so, with Start when it runs on this computer.

Privacy setup

Until you have answered them, a Complete setup row sits above Settings in the sidebar. It walks you through every privacy choice that applies to you, one question to a screen: adult content, a password, caching, blur, safe mode, filtered words, search indexing, and secure wipe with its panic password. Each answer is saved as you give it, and you can leave and come back where you left off. Every choice can be changed later in Settings.

Your first remix

  1. Open Media, press the folder name at the top and choose Output. Pick a picture ComfyUI made.
  2. Press the bar under its thumbnail to open Remix. The workflow inside the file is offered as Inherit, alongside the workflows in your library.
  3. Change the prompt, press Next until you reach Run, and press it. Its outputs appear as thumbnails as each one finishes, and the job is on the Jobs page.

A new install comes with ten workflows switched on, and sample pictures in your Favorites to try them on. Press Enable on one and the app checks it against your ComfyUI, lists anything missing, and once nothing is, runs it in one click.

Updates

The app checks 10 seconds after it opens and every 4 hours, downloads a new version in the background, and asks before it restarts: Restart now or Later, which installs it the next time you quit. It never offers to restart while jobs are running. The card at the top of Home checks, installs and restarts on a press, from the computer or from your phone. Installed only for me, updates are silent; installed for everyone on the computer, each asks for administrator approval.

An update replaces the program and nothing else: your data folder, your ComfyUI and its models are left alone. When the Acceptable Use Policy changes, the app asks you to agree to the new version once. The check sends GitHub your IP address and the fact that a copy is checking, nothing about your media, prompts or settings.

Let an AI client drive it

Settings → Connect an AI client lets a program on this computer, such as Claude Code or Grok Build, use WorkflowRemix through the Model Context Protocol: change ComfyUI's settings, check and enable workflows, and build and run Studios projects. It is local only, behind a token shown once, and nothing reaches it from the internet: an app's hosted connector cannot connect. Every client is told to leave your media alone. Off until you switch it on.

Finding your data later

File → Show Data Folder opens the folder you chose. Uninstalling the app leaves it in place.
