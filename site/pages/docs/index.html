Documentation

WorkflowRemix is a desktop app that sits beside your ComfyUI. It generates nothing itself: it is a library and a remote control for the ComfyUI you already run, with your models and your custom nodes.

How it works

ComfyUI writes the workflow that made a file into the file itself. WorkflowRemix reads it back: so it can show you a library you can search by prompt, turn any file's workflow into a form with the controls that matter, and queue a new run on your own ComfyUI with whatever you changed. What it embeds in the files it makes is exactly what ran, so a picture opened again in ComfyUI makes the same picture.

The pieces on your computer

PieceWhat it does
The windowWhat you see and click. Every image, script and font comes from the app itself; a link to anywhere else opens in your system browser.
The serverSearches your library, turns workflows into forms, reads every run with the built-in safeguard and sends it to ComfyUI. It answers only its own window, and keeps running in the tray when the window is closed, so your phone can still reach it.
Your data folderSettings, workflows, prompts, rules, Studios' projects and, unless you put it elsewhere, your media. You choose where it lives on the first launch.
ComfyUILoads the models and renders the images, video and audio, on your graphics card. Yours, installed separately; a Docker install works as well as a native one.
Ollama, if you set it upThe AI agent: a language model on your own computer that writes prompts and builds projects by calling the app's own tools.
Tailscale, if you switch it onLets your phone reach the app over your own private network: From your phone.

Two things to know before you start

You need a working ComfyUI of your own: WorkflowRemix does not install one, though its setup page checks yours and says what it is missing. And it is for lawful use by adults: installing it means agreeing to the Acceptable Use Policy.
