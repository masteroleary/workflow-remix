Studios

The part of the app for making short videos out of characters: a project is sequences of clips, each clip a render of its own, joined into one video when you press Run. It renders with MiniMax H3 or LTX-2.5, draws its pictures with Krea, and has an AI agent on your own computer that can build a whole project with you.

Its pages are under Studios in the sidebar: Projects, Characters, Templates and Preferences. Each video engine has a switch in Settings → Studios.

Watch it being done: a project built by the AI from one sentence, a project's pictures set up on its Images tab, and a character swapped into a finished video.

Start a project

Press + on the Projects page. Every new project starts on a setup screen: the engine, the frame (wide 16:9, tall 9:16 or square), the resolution (Small, Standard or Max) and, on MiniMax H3, Turbo. Then:

  • Create New makes a project with one clip and one shot, filled in enough to render.
  • Create with agent opens the AI agent beside an empty project, waiting for you to describe the video.
  • Build from Prompt hands the agent a prompt you wrote or found, to turn into characters, clips and shots. You choose whether it draws storyboards to pick from, writes picture prompts, or makes no pictures at all.
  • Import turns a prompt written for H3, Sora, Veo or Kling into a project in one go, with or without the agent.
  • Add a sample: Neon Tokyo Heist (an action scene, on H3 and on LTX-2.5), The Resonant Bloom (above) and The Dressing Room (a costume change, with a storyboard). Press Enable on one and the app checks that the workflows it needs are installed, then Run makes the pictures, writes the prompts and renders the video.

A project card's Duplicate makes a full copy, files and all, in a folder named after the copy. Before a project's first render you can change its engine; after it, duplicate it onto the other engine instead.

Sequences, clips and shots

  • A shot is one camera shot: its prompt, its length in seconds, an optional line of dialogue, and an optional reference picture.
  • A clip is one render, up to fifteen seconds on H3 and twenty on LTX-2.5. Its scene is said once for all its shots, and it casts its characters as Subject 1, Subject 2 and so on. Each clip has its own engine, LoRAs, seed, takes and first and last frames, and can start on the last frame of the clip before it.
  • A sequence is clips in order, with a soundscape and a score. Run joins every sequence that is not set to Omit from Run into the project's video.

The project page shows every sequence as a section of a timeline, each piece as wide as its seconds, against the engine's limit. Press a clip or a shot and its form opens underneath, with a breadcrumb of where you are and what you can do there. Drag pieces to reorder them, or turn a shot into a clip of its own with Convert to Clip. Rename anything and the folder its files are saved in is renamed to match.

Characters

A character has a description, a voice and a picture, and lives in your library to cast in any project. The voice builder offers words for pitch, texture, pace and the rest, and says how far apart two voices are, so nobody sounds like somebody else. Each character also has assets: a face close-up, a full-body picture and named looks, each drawn from their own picture so the face stays theirs.

  • Regen Refs draws every picture of a character again from their new picture, storyboards included.
  • Swap on a cast card puts another character in their place across the whole project: every clip, frame and storyboard picture that showed them, their name in the words if you like, and the pictures drawn from them made again, in the order each was drawn.

The Images tab

Every picture a render takes, its first frame, each shot's reference and its last frame, as a timeline running down the page. Each picture is made one of five ways:

  • Character: one of the clip's cast, their own picture or a look.
  • Krea: drawn from a prompt, or with Reference Character drawn from a character so it is really them.
  • Edit Prev: an edit of a picture before it, saying only what changes.
  • Frame: any frame of a take you have rendered, stepped to frame by frame.
  • Storyboard: a picture from one of the project's storyboards, linked so it changes when the storyboard does.

Between two clips, Bridge makes the end of one and the start of the next the same picture, so the joined video flows. Each clip's Prompt shows what its render will send, to read while writing a picture's prompt, and Regenerate lights up when a picture's prompt has changed since it was drawn.

Storyboards

A storyboard is a run of pictures, each drawn from the one before, so a character stays the same person in the same place while the pictures walk them into the next pose. Build draws them in order; Make clips turns each neighbouring pair into a clip that starts on one and ends on the next.

LoRAs

The LoRAs tab is a table: LoRAs down the side, a column per clip, and a strength where they meet. A LoRA made for another engine is off in that clip's cell. LoRAs your ComfyUI has for the project's engine are suggested, a search box finds them by their tags, and the ⓘ beside one shows its notes and the settings it recommends, which its renders use. Under each column a total warns past 1.5, the point at which, in our tests, stacked LoRAs start to pull a character off their pictures.

Takes and Run

  • Render Clip renders one clip on its own as a quick Draft or a Final. Each render is a take with its own seed. When a draft looks right, Lock this seed, and Make final renders that same take at full quality.
  • Run renders every clip final, in order, with its locked seed, and joins them: a clip that carries on waits for the one before and starts on its last frame. Run on another project meanwhile is queued.
  • Join takes strings the clips' takes together into a preview, with nothing rendered.
  • Under each video, the prompt follows along as it plays, the shot on screen lit. Press a shot to jump the video there.

The AI agent

An agent that runs on your own computer, through Ollama, set up in Settings → Ollama Toolcall Agent. Nothing you say to it is logged or kept. Press AI on a project and it opens as a panel beside the page, and stays open as you move around the app.

  • It can do what you can: write and rework characters, clips and shots, cast your own characters, draw storyboards, pick pictures into frames, set LoRAs at their recommended strengths, and offer renders. Every change comes back as a card with before and after, and nothing changes until you apply it.
  • It works in steps: storyboard, clips, images, LoRAs and drafts. Follow the AI takes the page to whatever it is changing, and Don't ask lets it carry on through the steps by itself, render the finals at the end, and stop when you say.
  • It checks its own work against what a run would really make, and says what would not come out as meant.
  • It remembers your preferences for every project, which you can read and edit in its settings.
  • It takes turns with ComfyUI on the graphics card, so a chat never fights a render for memory.

Models strong at tool use make the best agents. The one we recommend is Qwen 3.6 27B (heretic v2, Q4_K_M), about 18 GB of graphics memory at a 16K context: ollama pull hf.co/llmfan46/Qwen3.6-27B-uncensored-heretic-v2-Native-MTP-Preserved-GGUF:Q4_K_M.

PicturesUp to nine references, named in the prompt, plus a first and last frameA first and last frame and up to three keyframes, pinned in time
SoundVoices, effects and music in the same passA soundtrack in the same pass

NeedsThe MiniMax H3 modelsThe LTX-2.5 models (gated: a free Hugging Face token), and 32 GB of graphics memory by Lightricks' own figure

Prompts are written the way each engine reads them, through templates on the Templates page: a cast and shots, H3's second-by-second timeline, H3's own schema, and LTX's one take or a take with cuts. Edit them or write your own with keywords like [clips] and [shots]. Pictures are drawn with Krea, or with MOSS-Image if you choose it in Studios → Preferences.

What it needs

  • A ComfyUI that can run the engine you pick, and Krea for the pictures. Enable lists anything missing, with a download where the app knows the file.
  • For the AI agent, optionally: Ollama with a model good at tool use, on your own computer.
  • Every prompt is read by the app's built-in safeguard before it is shown or run (see Privacy and security).
