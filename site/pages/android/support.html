Android app support

The WorkflowRemix app puts WorkflowRemix, running on your own computer, on your Android phone or tablet, privately: it reaches your computer through your own Tailscale network, and keeps what it shows behind your device’s lock.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet with Android 12 or later, with a screen lock set. The app keeps its pairing behind your screen lock, so it cannot pair without one.
  • WorkflowRemix on your computer, from Downloads, kept up to date. It works with the ComfyUI you already run.
  • A Tailscale account. A free personal account is enough. You do not need the Tailscale app on your device: the app has its own connection.
  • Remote access switched on in WorkflowRemix, with a password of at least 12 characters.

Getting started

  1. On your computer, in WorkflowRemix, open Settings → Remote access and switch it on. Press Show pairing code.
  2. Point your device’s Camera app or Google Lens at the code and tap the link that appears. Or copy the link shown under the code and paste it on the app’s Pair screen.
  3. Check your computer’s name on the confirmation and tap Pair.
  4. The first time, the app asks you to sign in to Tailscale: tap Sign in, and Tailscale’s page opens in your browser. Use the same account as your computer, then come back to the app. If your tailnet approves new devices by hand, approve the one whose name starts workflowremix- in Tailscale’s admin console.
  5. Sign in with your WorkflowRemix password.

The code on your computer’s screen is only an address. It holds no password and no key, so a photo of it lets nobody in.

Why some things are greyed out on the device

From a phone or tablet, WorkflowRemix lets you browse, view, upload, favourite and delete your media, start and cancel runs, and use your prompts. It does not let a device change any setting, start ComfyUI, restart, restore a backup or look through the computer’s folders: those say Change this on the computer. That is on purpose. Anyone who learned your remote password should not be able to change your computer, only use the app.

Some images may be hidden. WorkflowRemix hides from phones and tablets the files its own word filter flags, and only a setting on the computer can show them. The filter matches the words you list against each file’s prompt; it does not look at the images.

Privacy

  • Lock: the app locks when you leave it (after a minute, by default) and opens with your fingerprint or your screen lock.
  • Screenshots blocked: screenshots, screen recordings, casting and the recent-apps preview show nothing of your library, and other apps cannot record the sound of a video playing in it.
  • Sign out after idle: after 8 hours away, by default, the app signs you out and empties its cache.
  • Wipe now: on the lock screen and in Pairing & privacy, removes everything the app keeps and signs it out of Tailscale. Your pairing stays, and you sign in again.
  • Typing: the keyboard is asked not to learn from what you type, and autofill stays out.
  • Copying: what you copy is marked sensitive, so the clipboard preview hides it. The app clears it after two minutes, or as soon as you come back to the app if you left it: Android lets only the app in front clear the clipboard.
  • Backups: nothing the app keeps is backed up or carried to a new device.
  • Nothing else: no analytics, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Removing a device

  • On the device: in Pairing & privacy, Forget this computer, then Sign out of Tailscale and remove this device, then uninstall the app. Uninstalling alone also removes everything the app keeps, but leaves the app’s device signed in to your tailnet until you remove it there.
  • If the device is lost: on your computer, use Sign out every device (Settings → Security), and remove the app’s workflowremix- device in Tailscale’s admin console.

Questions

Do I need the Tailscale app? No. The app has its own connection, which nothing else on your device can use. If the Tailscale app is installed, the app does not use it.

Does it work away from home? Yes, anywhere your device has a connection.

Is my computer’s name public? When you switch on HTTPS in Tailscale, your computer’s Tailscale name is written into public certificate logs, permanently. Rename the computer in Tailscale first if its name says anything about you.

Why doesn’t the app remember my password? On purpose: it never stores or fills a password.

Can Tailscale’s support help with the app? Not with the app itself: it is built with Tailscale’s logging switched off, so Tailscale’s support cannot see its logs. Ask us instead.

How do I delete my Tailscale account? Pairing & privacy → Delete your Tailscale account… opens Tailscale’s own page for it.

Contact

  • Privately: in WorkflowRemix on your computer, open Support → Report a problem, and say in the note that it is about the Android app. You see the whole report before it is sent, and our answer appears under Your reports.
  • In public: open an issue at github.com/masteroleary/workflow-remix. Issues are public, so leave out anything private.
