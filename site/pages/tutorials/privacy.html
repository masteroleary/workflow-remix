Keep your library private

A media library is personal. Here is every way WorkflowRemix keeps yours that way, in under two minutes.

The steps

  1. Blur in the sidebar blurs every picture, the viewer too, and comes back on whenever the app goes out of sight.
  2. The shield hides files your word list or an NSFW tag flags; its demo mode also hides models, LoRAs and workflows tagged NSFW.
  3. Settings → Privacy holds media caching and the search-indexing check; Review privacy choices walks through them one question at a time.
  4. Settings → Secure wipe erases what you tick, in the app, and a panic password on the lock screen erases instead of letting anyone in.
  5. Log out, and the lock screen says nothing about what is inside.

Everything that leaves the computer, and what does not, is in Privacy and security.
