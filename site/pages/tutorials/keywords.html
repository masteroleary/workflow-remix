Make variations with keywords

One prompt, many pictures. Write the prompt once with [keywords] in it, tick the answers you want for each, and every combination is rendered.

The steps

  1. Add a collection on the Prompts page. Each category in it is a keyword, and each row on a category's shelf is an answer that keyword can take. A row may hold another keyword: here every hairstyle carries [haircolor].
  2. Open Remix on a picture and write the prompt once, with a keyword in brackets wherever the picture should change.
  3. On the Keywords tab, pick the collection and tick the answers for each keyword. The panel says how many prompts that makes before anything runs.
  4. Read the prompts in the preview under the rows, every one in full, and untick any you do not want.
  5. On the Run tab, choose how many runs of each prompt, and press Run. Each picture appears as its run finishes, and every job is on the Jobs page.

More on keywords, rules, captures and collections is in The prompt library.
