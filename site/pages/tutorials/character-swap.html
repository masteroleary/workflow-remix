Swap a character into a video

Keep a video's scene, camera, motion and sound, and put someone else in it. Open the video in Remix, switch the workflow to Character Swap, pick a picture of who goes in, and describe them.

The steps

  1. On the Workflows page, press Enable on Minimax H3 Character Swap. It checks that every model and node it needs is installed, and offers Download for any that are missing.
  2. In Media, find the video to use as the guide: the clip whose scene, camera and movement you want to keep. Press the bar under it to open Remix.
  3. Pick Minimax H3 Character Swap in the Workflow menu. Its video box holds the clip you opened; Browse picks another.
  4. In its picture box, Browse to a picture of who goes in, and in the prompt describe them head to toe, starting with whether they are a man or a woman.
  5. Keep the guide to 30 seconds or less, and shorter if your machine has less memory. Its length is taken from the video; untick Use reference video length to type a shorter one.
  6. Press Run, and follow the job on Jobs. The new video lands in ComfyUI's output.

More on the form, its boxes and its runs is in Remixing a file.
