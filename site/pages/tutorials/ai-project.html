Build a video project from one sentence

Describe a short video, and the AI agent writes the project: its character, her voice, two clips and the lines she says. A picture you already made starts it, and Studios renders the clips and joins them into one video.

The steps

  1. Set up the AI agent in Settings → Ollama Toolcall Agent. It finds a running Ollama by itself and picks a model that can use tools.
  2. Under Studios, open Projects and choose + → Build from Prompt. Pick the frame, the resolution and Turbo, and what the agent does about pictures: storyboards, picture prompts, or none.
  3. Say what to build, as you would to a person: who is in it, where it happens, and what they say. Press Build it.
  4. The agent works in a panel beside the page, step by step, and its changes apply as it goes. A voice sample keeps a character's voice the same from clip to clip.
  5. To start a clip from a picture you already have, open the project's Images tab, switch on that clip's first frame, and pick the picture from the library.
  6. Enable the project and press Run. Every clip renders in full, and they are joined into one video in the project's folder.
