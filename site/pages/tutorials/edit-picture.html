Edit a picture with words

Some changes are easier to say than to draw. An edit workflow takes the picture you opened and an instruction, and keeps everything you did not ask it to change.

The steps

  1. Open the picture in the viewer and press Remix.
  2. Pick Krea Image Edit from the workflow menu. It takes the picture you opened as its input, and opens on its own instruction rather than the picture's prompt.
  3. Say what to change and what to keep: “change her outfit to a knitted sweater and a red scarf, and the background to a snowy village at dusk. Keep her face, hair and earrings.”
  4. Run it. With no keywords in the instruction, Next goes straight to the Run tab.
  5. Remix the result from the bar under it, and write the next instruction. It opens on the same workflow, with the instruction that made it.

More on the form and switching workflows is in Remix; the edit workflows are listed in Workflows and models.
