Check a workflow, then run it

A ComfyUI workflow only runs if every custom node and model it names is installed. WorkflowRemix checks that for you before it offers the workflow, and says what is missing.

The steps

  1. On the Workflows page, press + and tick a workflow: the app's own, or one from your ComfyUI's folder.
  2. The check starts by itself, ticking off every custom node, model, LoRA, VAE and text encoder against your ComfyUI.
  3. Anything missing is listed under the folder it belongs in, with Download where the app knows the file, Find manually, and Copy Agent ToolCall for exact install steps.
  4. Everything found: the sheet says what Run will do, the pictures it feeds in and the prompt it sends. Press Run, and watch it on the Jobs page.

More on the check, downloads and the workflows that come with the app is in Workflows and models.
