Give every frame and shot its picture

The Images tab in H3 Studio lays out every picture a project's video uses, in the order the video meets them: each clip's first frame, its shots' reference pictures and its last frame. Each one can come from a character, a Krea prompt, an edit of the picture before it, a frame of a take, or a storyboard.

The steps

  1. In H3 Studio, open a project and its Images tab. Each clip is a section, its pictures in the order the video meets them.
  2. A frame is a polaroid: the video starts or ends on exactly that picture. A reference is a plain picture that guides the look of its clip. Untick On to leave one out.
  3. Pick each picture's way: a Character's own picture, a Krea prompt, Edit of Last (an edit of the picture just before it), a Frame of a clip's take, or a Storyboard picture.
  4. Storyboard points at one of your storyboard's pictures rather than copying it: draw that picture again on the Storyboard tab and the new one is used here too.
  5. Frame takes a frame of a take. Step with the arrows under the picture, ten frames or one at a time, or press Where it ends. On a clip's first frame, Last, as rendered starts it wherever the clip before ends.
  6. Between two clips, Bridge makes the first one's last frame and the next one's first a single picture. Break gives each its own again.
  7. When a clip is rendered again, a frame taken from its earlier take says so, and Use where the new take ends takes the new take's last frame.

More on projects, clips and their takes is in H3 Studio.
