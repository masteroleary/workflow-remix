Turn a picture into a video, then cut it

A still picture becomes the first frame of a short clip, with its own sound. Then the viewer and Media cut it up: trim, split, save its last frame, join clips and lay its frames out on one picture.

The steps

  1. On the Workflows page, press Enable on Minimax H3 Video. It checks that every model and node it needs is installed.
  2. In Media, open Remix on a picture and pick Minimax H3 Video in the Workflow menu. The picture becomes the video's first frame.
  3. Write what happens: how the people move, how the camera moves, and what we hear. Say "No one speaks" when nobody should, or the model may make up words.
  4. Add a LoRA by searching for it under the LoRAs list, and put its trigger words in the prompt. The video uses Camera Motion, whose prompt starts with camera motion.
  5. Press Run. With Turbo on, a five-second clip takes about a minute. Open it from the Run tab to watch it with its sound.
  6. The viewer's ⋯ menu has Trim video, Split video and Last frame. The new clips are saved beside the one they came from, and Last frame saves a picture to start the next clip from.
  7. In Media, press Select and pick clips: Merge clips joins them in the order you set, and Frame sheet makes one picture of a clip's frames.

More on the viewer and the media grid is in Browsing your media.
