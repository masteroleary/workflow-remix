# WorkflowRemix Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

WorkflowRemix is a tool for browsing, organising and re-running your own AI-generated
media. It runs on your computer, with AI models, LoRAs and workflows that you choose and
install yourself. This policy says what you may not use it for. By installing or using
WorkflowRemix, you agree to it.

"We" and "us" mean the copyright holder of WorkflowRemix (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). "You"
means anyone who installs or uses it.

## Use it lawfully

- **Legal use only.** You may use WorkflowRemix only in ways that are legal where you live
  and wherever you share what you make with it.
- **Adults only.** You must be at least 18 years old, or the age of majority where you
  live if that is higher.
- **You are responsible for what you make.** That covers every prompt you write, every
  model, LoRA and workflow you run, and everything you generate, keep or share.
  WorkflowRemix runs on your own machine: we do not host, see or review your media, and
  we cannot vouch for what your models produce.

## Never allowed

There is zero tolerance for any of the following.

1. **Sexual content involving minors.** Any sexual or sexualised depiction of a person
   under 18, or of anyone who appears to be under 18. This applies whether the person is
   real or fictional, and whether the image is photorealistic, drawn, anime or otherwise
   stylised, generated or edited. It includes:
   - child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form;
   - prompts, workflows, models or LoRAs made or used to produce it;
   - using images of real children as input for sexual content.
2. **Intimate images without consent.** Nude, sexual or intimate depictions of a real,
   identifiable person who has not consented, including deepfakes and face or body swaps.
3. **Harm to real people.** Impersonation, fraud, defamation, harassment, threats, or
   content made to bully, extort or intimidate anyone.
4. **Exploitation and extremism.** Content that promotes or facilitates sexual violence,
   human trafficking or terrorism.
5. **Other illegal content.** Anything that is illegal to make, possess or share where you
   are.
6. **Other people's rights.** Infringing copyright, trademark or privacy rights. This
   includes breaking the licence terms of a model, LoRA, checkpoint or workflow you use:
   many carry use restrictions of their own (the OpenRAIL family of licences, for
   example), and you must follow them.
7. **Getting around safeguards.** Disabling, bypassing or tampering with any safety
   feature of WorkflowRemix.

## Adult content

This policy does not prohibit lawful content involving adults who are, and clearly appear
to be, adults. Keep it within the law where you live and within the rules above, and away
from anyone who has not chosen to see it.

## If you come across illegal material

If you encounter child sexual abuse material, do not copy, save or share it. Report it:

- **United States:** the NCMEC CyberTipline, [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org/)
- **United Kingdom:** the Internet Watch Foundation, [report.iwf.org.uk](https://report.iwf.org.uk/)
- **Elsewhere:** your national hotline, listed by [INHOPE](https://www.inhope.org/), or
  the police.

## Enforcement

- **Your licence ends.** Breaking this policy ends your licence to use WorkflowRemix,
  automatically and without notice. You must then stop using it and delete every copy you
  have.
- **No more support.** We may refuse you support, updates and future purchases.
- **Reporting.** We report apparent child sexual exploitation that comes to our attention
  to the authorities, and we cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is published with each release, and using
WorkflowRemix after an update means you accept the updated version.
